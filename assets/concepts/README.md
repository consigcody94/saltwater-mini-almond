# AlmondLab visual concepts

These images are design visualizations generated on 12 August 2026. They are
not photographs, experimental observations, engineering drawings, or evidence
that any genetic construct works in almond.

## 01 — Contained greenhouse concept

Shows the proposed physical research layout: compact almond trees in sealed
lysimeters; source-water pretreatment and reverse osmosis; nutrient blending;
separate irrigation loops; captured drainage and condensate; and isolated
concentrate storage. The critical design rule is visible: saline source water
is treated before irrigation, and drainage never reaches agricultural soil.

## 02 — Six-gene mechanism map

Shows where each proposed module is hypothesized to act. It deliberately marks
the `SbSOS1` xylem-loading route as a risk and distinguishes ROS control from
ion removal. Every mechanism remains a hypothesis requiring almond validation.

## 03 — Virtual-lab dashboard demo

Shows the intended software experience: candidate gates, the closed-loop salt
ledger, a mini-tree compartment model, and uncertainty/sensitivity displays.
All candidate states are `NOT EVALUATED`. The values visible in this concept
are illustrative interface content, not outputs from the AlmondLab simulator.

## 04 — Four-stream closed-loop greenhouse, version 2

Adds explicit, color-separated feed seawater, clean product water, captured
drainage, and brine-concentrate paths. It also shows remineralization/blending,
condensate recovery, sealed root-zone containers, secondary brine containment,
and EC/Na/Cl/flow/tank/environment sensing. This is an architectural research
concept, not a construction drawing or a biological result.

## 05 — Replicated experimental bay, version 3

Shows how the comparative experiment could be arranged inside the contained
greenhouse: a non-engineered control and hypothesis groups `C1` through `C6`,
with multiple trees per block, individual sealed 40-liter root-zone containers,
secondary containment, sensors, and captured drainage. Feed seawater, treated
and remineralized irrigation, drainage return, and concentrate remain separate.
The candidate labels are experimental group names only; they do not imply that
any construct is safe, effective, or superior.

Built-in OpenAI image generation was used. The exact production prompts are in
[PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md).

## Revision policy

These are evolving concepts. Revisions use sibling filenames such as `-v2`
rather than overwriting prior assets. A scientific image is revised when a
verified interface, compartment, experimental allocation, chemistry value, or
decision label changes. Once the simulator is operational, dashboard numbers
must be rendered from a manifest-listed synthetic run rather than copied from
this illustrative UI mockup.
