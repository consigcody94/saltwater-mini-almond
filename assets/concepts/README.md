# AlmondLab visual concepts

These images are design visualizations generated on 12 August 2026. They are
not photographs, experimental observations, engineering drawings, or evidence
that any genetic construct works in almond.

## 01 — Contained greenhouse concept

Shows the proposed physical research layout: compact almond trees in sealed
lysimeters; source-water pretreatment and reverse osmosis; nutrient blending;
separate irrigation loops; captured drainage and condensate; and isolated
concentrate storage. The critical design rule is visible: saline source water
is treated before irrigation, and drainage never reaches agricultural soil.

## 02 — Six-gene mechanism map

Shows where each proposed module is hypothesized to act. It deliberately marks
the `SbSOS1` xylem-loading route as a risk and distinguishes ROS control from
ion removal. Every mechanism remains a hypothesis requiring almond validation.

## 03 — Virtual-lab dashboard demo

Shows the intended software experience: candidate gates, the closed-loop salt
ledger, a mini-tree compartment model, and uncertainty/sensitivity displays.
All candidate states are `NOT EVALUATED`. The values visible in this concept
are illustrative interface content, not outputs from the AlmondLab simulator.

Built-in OpenAI image generation was used. The exact production prompts are in
[PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md).
