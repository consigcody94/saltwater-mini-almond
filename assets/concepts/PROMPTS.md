## 01 — Contained greenhouse

```text

Asset type: publication concept plate / greenhouse visualization
Primary request: Create a highly realistic architectural cutaway visualization of a contained research greenhouse for compact almond trees using treated saline source water.
Scene/backdrop: California research facility in a dry Central Valley landscape, viewed as a wide three-quarter cutaway showing both greenhouse interior and the adjacent water-treatment equipment.
Subject: rows of healthy 1.2–1.8 meter dwarf almond trees in individual sealed 40-liter lysimeter containers; each container has drip irrigation, drainage tubing, weight sensor, and a separate labeled-color research loop. Show flowering and a small number of almonds on several trees, while keeping the trees botanically recognizable as almonds.
Engineering details: source-water tank feeds an ion-specific pretreatment/RO skid; permeate goes to nutrient blending and irrigation reservoirs; all drainage returns through a below-bench pipe manifold; condensate is captured; concentrate goes to a sealed disposal tank; secondary containment floor has a shallow curb and leak sensors. Include compact sensor cabinet and sample ports. Make it visually obvious that untreated seawater never pours directly onto roots and that no saline drainage reaches soil.
Style/medium: photorealistic scientific architectural visualization with clean, credible engineering, not science fiction.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape, wide cutaway, readable equipment relationships, human researcher for scale only.
Lighting/mood: bright diffuse greenhouse daylight, calm, credible, optimistic but restrained.
Text (verbatim): "CONCEPT — NOT BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE"
Constraints: render the exact caution text once in a clean footer; scientifically plausible plumbing; closed-loop containment; no corporate logos; no impossible giant fruit; no ocean water visibly touching plant roots; no discharge pipe to land; no hazmat drama; no watermark beyond the requested caution footer.
Avoid: fantasy glowing plants, oversized trees, hydroponic roots floating in seawater, futuristic city aesthetic.
```
