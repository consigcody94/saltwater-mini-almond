What AlmondLab could look like

Three linked views of the proposed research program: the contained greenhouse, the mechanism tournament, and the auditable virtual laboratory.

1. Contained greenhouse

Treated source water enters separate experimental loops. Each compact tree sits in a sealed lysimeter; drainage and condensate are captured, while concentrate is measured and isolated for authorized disposal.

Untreated seawater is not applied directly to almond roots.

2. Six-gene mechanism tournament

Each construct is tied to a measurable physiological mechanism. The diagram preserves the central risk: moving sodium out of one root cell is not useful if it simply raises xylem and shoot sodium.

These are hypotheses, not evidence of engineered-almond performance.

3. Virtual-lab dashboard demo

The software joins candidate gates to water and ion ledgers, root/shoot compartments, uncertainty, sensitivity checks, evidence labels, and a reproducible run manifest.

Visible values are illustrative interface content; every biological candidate remains not evaluated.
